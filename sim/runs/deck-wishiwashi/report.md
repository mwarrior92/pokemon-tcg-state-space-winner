## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 46% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 22% of prizes; Full Moon Rondo (Lillie's Clefairy ex) – 18% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 4; 0.40 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: attach Water Energy to Wishiwashi (46%), retreat (44%), play Ultra Ball (44%), play Lillie's Determination (41%), bench Latias ex (41%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Water Energy (4.5 uses per won game vs 4.2 per lost game); Psychic Energy (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.8 per lost game); Telepathic Psychic Energy (1.3 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Lillie's Determination (1.5 uses per won game vs 1.3 per lost game); Dawn (0.7 uses per won game vs 0.5 per lost game); Ciphermaniac's Codebreaking (0.4 uses per won game vs 0.3 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Latias ex (0.8 vs 0.6); Crushing Hammer (1.2 vs 1.0); Prism Tower (0.2 vs 0.0); Buddy-Buddy Poffin (1.0 vs 0.9) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (47 turns; still there the next turn 57% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against N's Zoroark (100%), Slowking (100%); weakest against Crustle (0%), Alakazam Dudunsparce (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
