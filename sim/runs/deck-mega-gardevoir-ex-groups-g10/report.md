## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 67% of prizes; Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 26% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 4% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 4; 0.40 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: attach Psychic Energy to Mega Kangaskhan ex (100%), ability Run Errand (Mega Kangaskhan ex) (73%), play Emcee's Hype (54%), retreat (48%), tool Counter Gain (46%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (5.6 uses per won game vs 4.0 per lost game); Emcee's Hype (1.9 uses per won game vs 1.4 per lost game); Counter Gain (1.5 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Rescue Board (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.7 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (0.7 uses per won game vs 0.3 per lost game); Kirlia (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.8 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Mega Kangaskhan ex (1.1 vs 1.1); Nincada (0.2 vs 0.2) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (51 turns; still there the next turn 34% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against Festival Lead (100%), Slowking (67%); weakest against Alakazam Dusknoir (0%), Alakazam Dudunsparce (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
