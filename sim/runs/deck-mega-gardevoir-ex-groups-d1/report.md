## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 75% of prizes; Overwhelming Pain (Trevenant) – 19% of prizes; Cursed Roots (Trevenant) – 4% of prizes. Build towards Mega Gardevoir ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 5; 0.19 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: play Poké Ball (52%), evolve Kirlia (48%), bench Tangela (47%), evolve Tangrowth (45%), play Pokémon Catcher (43%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (3.9 uses per won game vs 2.4 per lost game); Kirlia (2.1 uses per won game vs 1.2 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (1.3 uses per won game vs 0.5 per lost game); Pokémon Catcher (1.7 uses per won game vs 1.1 per lost game); Switch (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.5 per lost game); Dawn (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Special Red Card (0.2 vs 0.1); Wondrous Patch (0.2 vs 0.2); Tangela (1.3 vs 1.3) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:behind ready:0-2 ko:no threat:yes exposed:none" (80 turns; still there the next turn 69% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out); avoid leaving the Active in Knock Out range (retreat, switch, or put a cheaper Pokémon in front); keep a way to take a prize every turn (gust for weak benched targets, damage spread).
- **Matchups.** Best against Festival Lead (50%), Dragapult Dusknoir (50%); weakest against Dhelmise (0%), Basic Box (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
