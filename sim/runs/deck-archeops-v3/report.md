## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 55% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 19% of prizes; Full Moon Rondo (Lillie's Clefairy ex) – 18% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 4; 0.39 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: play Ultra Ball (50%), ability Run Errand (Mega Kangaskhan ex) (48%), retreat (39%), play Antique Plume Fossil (33%), play Boss's Orders (25%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Telepathic Psychic Energy (1.6 uses per won game vs 0.9 per lost game); Fighting Energy (2.3 uses per won game vs 1.6 per lost game); Psychic Energy (1.3 uses per won game vs 0.7 per lost game); Crispin (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.5 per lost game); Poké Pad (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Lillie's Determination (1.3 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Crushing Hammer (1.0 vs 1.0); Night Stretcher (0.4 vs 0.3); Latias ex (0.5 vs 0.5) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (117 turns; still there the next turn 68% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against Dragapult Dusknoir (100%), Dragapult Blaziken (100%); weakest against Crustle (25%), Alakazam Dusknoir (25%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
