## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 57% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 18% of prizes; Full Moon Rondo (Lillie's Clefairy ex) – 16% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 5; 0.31 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: retreat (44%), ability Run Errand (Mega Kangaskhan ex) (43%), play Boss's Orders (25%), play Antique Plume Fossil (25%), play Poké Pad (21%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Fighting Energy (3.0 uses per won game vs 1.6 per lost game); Telepathic Psychic Energy (1.7 uses per won game vs 1.1 per lost game); Lillie's Determination (1.4 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Psychic Energy (0.6 uses per won game vs 0.3 per lost game); Crispin (0.9 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Boss's Orders (1.2 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Crushing Hammer (1.2 vs 0.9); Night Stretcher (0.3 vs 0.2); Special Red Card (0.2 vs 0.1); Unfair Stamp (0.2 vs 0.1) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (128 turns; still there the next turn 67% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against Dragapult Dusknoir (100%), Slowking (86%); weakest against Crustle (0%), Basic Box (20%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
