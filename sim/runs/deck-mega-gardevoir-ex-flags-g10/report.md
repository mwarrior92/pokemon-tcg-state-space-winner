## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 80% of prizes; Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 17% of prizes; Psyshot (Kirlia) – 2% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 3; 0.40 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: attach Psychic Energy to Mega Kangaskhan ex (132%), ability Run Errand (Mega Kangaskhan ex) (98%), play Emcee's Hype (53%), bench Mega Kangaskhan ex (48%), bench Ralts (46%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (5.9 uses per won game vs 4.2 per lost game); Mega Kangaskhan ex (2.0 uses per won game vs 1.3 per lost game); Rocky Fighting Energy (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.4 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.5 per lost game); Emcee's Hype (1.8 uses per won game vs 1.3 per lost game); Ralts (1.8 uses per won game vs 1.4 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Applin (0.4 vs 0.3); Dipplin (0.1 vs 0.1) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (42 turns; still there the next turn 27% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against Slowking (56%), Hydrapple (50%); weakest against Alakazam Dudunsparce (0%), N's Zoroark (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
