- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 72% of prizes; Full Moon Rondo (Lillie's Clefairy ex) – 10% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 9% of prizes. Build towards Mega Gardevoir ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 5; 0.37 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: retreat retreat (56%), play Buddy-Buddy Poffin (56%), play Poké Pad (54%), attach Psychic Energy to Ralts (45%), bench Munkidori (39%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (4.3 uses per won game vs 3.5 per lost game); Poké Pad (2.0 uses per won game vs 1.5 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (0.8 uses per won game vs 0.3 per lost game); Lillie's Determination (1.7 uses per won game vs 1.3 per lost game); Crispin (1.2 uses per won game vs 0.9 per lost game); Ultra Ball (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.8 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Crushing Hammer (1.5 vs 1.1); Unfair Stamp (0.3 vs 0.1); Budew (0.6 vs 0.4); Latias ex (0.6 vs 0.5) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:behind ready:0-1 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (32 turns; still there the next turn 48% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out); keep a way to take a prize every turn (gust for weak benched targets, damage spread).
- **Matchups.** Best against Festival Lead (83%), Dragapult Dusknoir (75%); weakest against Crustle (25%), Basic Box (30%).
