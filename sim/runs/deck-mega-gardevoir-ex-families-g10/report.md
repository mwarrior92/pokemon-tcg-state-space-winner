## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Rapid-Fire Combo (Mega Kangaskhan ex) – 49% of prizes; Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 41% of prizes; Blaze Blast (Incineroar ex) – 2% of prizes. Build towards Mega Kangaskhan ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 4; 0.24 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: tool Air Balloon (60%), play Carmine (39%), ability Run Errand (Mega Kangaskhan ex) (33%), attach Psychic Energy to Mega Kangaskhan ex (29%), bench Ralts (29%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (4.9 uses per won game vs 2.4 per lost game); Water Energy (1.6 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Kirlia (1.4 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Air Balloon (1.7 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (0.9 uses per won game vs 0.2 per lost game); Ralts (1.2 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Snivy (0.6 vs 0.4); Growing [G] Energy (0.2 vs 0.1); Ignition Energy (0.1 vs 0.1); Finizen (0.4 vs 0.3) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:behind ready:0-2 ko:no threat:yes exposed:none" (49 turns; still there the next turn 46% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out); avoid leaving the Active in Knock Out range (retreat, switch, or put a cheaper Pokémon in front); keep a way to take a prize every turn (gust for weak benched targets, damage spread).
- **Matchups.** Best against Slowking (33%), Festival Lead (33%); weakest against Dragapult Blaziken (0%), Alakazam Dusknoir (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
