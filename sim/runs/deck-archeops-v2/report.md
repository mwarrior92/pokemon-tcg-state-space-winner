## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Full Moon Rondo (Lillie's Clefairy ex) – 52% of prizes; Eon Blade (Latias ex) – 32% of prizes; Rock Throw (Archeops) – 8% of prizes. Build towards Lillie's Clefairy ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 4; 0.35 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: play Ultra Ball (60%), retreat (53%), play Antique Plume Fossil (34%), play Buddy-Buddy Poffin (31%), bench Latias ex (29%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (1.6 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Fighting Energy (2.2 uses per won game vs 1.8 per lost game); Crispin (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Telepathic Psychic Energy (1.2 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Antique Plume Fossil (1.2 uses per won game vs 0.9 per lost game); Poké Pad (1.4 uses per won game vs 1.2 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Crushing Hammer (1.2 vs 1.0); Latias ex (0.7 vs 0.5); Special Red Card (0.1 vs 0.1); Unfair Stamp (0.1 vs 0.1) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:even ready:0-2 ko:no threat:no exposed:none" (76 turns; still there the next turn 60% of the time). To get out: get a second attacker ready earlier (bench and power up the next attacker before the first is Knocked Out).
- **Matchups.** Best against Dhelmise (75%), Dragapult (71%); weakest against Crustle (0%), Basic Box (10%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
