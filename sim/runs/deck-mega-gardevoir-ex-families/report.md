## Recommended play style

- **Win condition.** Prizes come mostly from Mega Symphonia (Mega Gardevoir ex) – 79% of prizes; Ear Force (Mega Audino ex) – 18% of prizes; Pitch-Black Fangs (Hydreigon) – 2% of prizes. Build towards Mega Gardevoir ex first.
- **Tempo.** First prize typically on its own turn 5; 0.25 prizes per own turn.
- **Opening (first two turns).** Most games: play Carmine (60%), attack Kaleidowaltz (56%), attach Psychic Energy to Mega Audino ex (53%), attach Psychic Energy to Ralts (43%), attach Psychic Energy to Scorbunny (40%).
- **Cards that show up in wins.** Psychic Energy (4.1 uses per won game vs 3.1 per lost game); Carmine (2.0 uses per won game vs 1.0 per lost game); Kirlia (1.4 uses per won game vs 0.6 per lost game); Mega Gardevoir ex (1.0 uses per won game vs 0.2 per lost game); Team Rocket's Transceiver (1.1 uses per won game vs 0.4 per lost game); Ralts (1.6 uses per won game vs 0.9 per lost game). Prioritise getting these into play / using them.
- **Cards that show up in losses.** Scorbunny (0.6 vs 0.3) – often a sign of playing from behind with them, or of a weak slot.
- **Where it gets stuck.** Most often "race:behind ready:2-2 ko:no threat:yes exposed:no" (26 turns; still there the next turn 32% of the time). To get out: avoid leaving the Active in Knock Out range (retreat, switch, or put a cheaper Pokémon in front); keep a way to take a prize every turn (gust for weak benched targets, damage spread).
- **Matchups.** Best against Slowking (56%), Dragapult Dusknoir (50%); weakest against Dragapult Blaziken (0%), Alakazam Dusknoir (0%).

## How to read this

- Games are full games from turn 1 against meta decks chosen by their share of the decklists, piloted by the same simulator policy on both sides. Set-up choices (opening Active and Bench) and the opponent's prompts during a turn are made at random.
- The result says how well this simulator plays the deck, not only how strong the deck is: decks whose plan relies on long ability chains are likely under-piloted. Compare a new deck with known meta decks tested the same way.
